Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information Studio Scout ("the tool"), operated by Emmanuel S. Lazaro Jr. ("we"), uses and how it is handled. Studio Scout uses YouTube API Services. YouTube data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the tool uses

Studio Scout reads public information from the YouTube Data API about a short list of public channels chosen by its operator:

  • channel names and public channel statistics;
  • video IDs, titles, descriptions, tags and upload dates;
  • public view, like and comment counts (never the text of comments, and never information about who viewed or liked a video).

The tool does not use cookies, tracking pixels or analytics, and this website collects no information from visitors.

No user accounts or Google sign-in

Studio Scout does not ask anyone to sign in with Google and does not access any private YouTube account data. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first, and you will be able to revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google security settings.

How information is used

Only to help the operator research their niche and plan content for their own channels: for example, to see which video topics and formats in the niche are popular, and to draft video ideas.

Storage and retention

  • Data is stored only on the operator's own computer. It is not uploaded to any other service.
  • Titles, descriptions, tags and other API text are deleted or refreshed within 30 days.
  • Public statistics are deleted within 30 days, unless YouTube has approved this tool under its additional policies for statistical data. In that case public statistics may be kept for up to 36 months, as those policies allow.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose YouTube data or any other information to third parties.

Deletion requests

If you own a channel and want its data removed from the tool, email eslazarojr@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
