Studio Scout

A private research tool that helps one independent creator plan better videos for their own YouTube channels.

Uses YouTube API Services. By using Studio Scout you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google Privacy Policy · Studio Scout Privacy Policy

What it does

  • Follows a short list of public YouTube channels that the creator picks by hand.
  • Reads public information about those channels' videos through the YouTube Data API: titles, descriptions, tags, upload dates and public view, like and comment counts.
  • Shows the creator which kinds of videos in their niche are getting views, and drafts content ideas for the creator's own channels from that research.

Who uses it

Studio Scout is a personal tool, run by its developer on their own computer for their own channels. It has no public sign-up, no user accounts and no advertising. No one else's YouTube account is connected to it, and it does not ask anyone to sign in with Google.

What it does not do

  • It does not post, comment, like, subscribe or change anything on YouTube.
  • It does not download videos or audio.
  • It does not collect personal information about viewers or other creators.
  • It does not sell or share YouTube data with anyone.

YouTube data
