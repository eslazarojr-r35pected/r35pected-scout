Studio Publisher

Uploads the creator's own finished videos to the creator's own YouTube channels, only after the creator approves each one.

Uses YouTube API Services. By using Studio Publisher you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google Privacy Policy · Revoke access in your Google security settings

Privacy policy and terms for Studio Publisher · Effective November 9, 2026

What it does

  • The creator, Emmanuel S. Lazaro Jr., signs in once with the Google account of each of their own YouTube channels and grants permission to upload videos.
  • When the creator approves a finished video in their private dashboard, Studio Publisher uploads it to that channel with the title, description and settings the creator approved, including "made for kids" where it applies and YouTube's altered or synthetic content disclosure.
  • It reads the signed-in channel's ID and name only to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

Who uses it

Only the creator, for their own channels. There is no public sign-up, and no one else's YouTube account is connected.

Google user data it uses

  • Permissions requested: upload videos (youtube.upload) and read the channel's basic details (youtube.readonly).
  • Stored: the sign-in tokens, only on the creator's own computer in a file only the tool can read, and the IDs of the videos it uploaded. Nothing else from the Google account is stored.
  • Never: no viewer data, comments, subscribers or analytics are collected, and nothing is shared with, sold to or uploaded to anyone else.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, or on request to eslazarojr@gmail.com, the stored tokens are deleted within 7 days.

Terms

Studio Publisher is a personal tool, provided as is. It follows the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies. It never posts anything the creator has not approved.
